Errata for the lecture videos

The lecture videos are published and are not re-recorded. When a check of the book found an error in a video, the book gives the correct version and the error is listed here. Each time links to the moment in the video. Errors marked serious change a number, a figure or a result that the rest of the lecture relies on. Small slips where the spoken point stands are not listed.

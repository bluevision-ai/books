Preface

This is the draft of Machine Learning: A Modelling Perspective, the companion book to Foundations of Machine Learning, a free lecture course from Bluevision AI. Each chapter covers one block of the course, and each section covers one lecture: section 1.1 is Session 01, and so on. The lectures give each idea as a picture and in speech; this book gives the full argument, the references and the exercises.

The perspective of the title is one question, asked of every method in the book: how much of a model comes from what we already know, and how much from what we measure. Chapter 1 opens that question, and each later chapter places its methods on the same axis.

The draft grows as the lectures are released. Chapters are revised before publication.

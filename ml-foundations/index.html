Foundations of Machine Learning

Vahid Seydi · a draft in progress · companion to the free lecture course Foundations of Machine Learning

This book is the written companion to the lecture course. Each chapter covers one block of the course and each section covers one lecture, so section 1.1 is Session 01. The lectures give each idea as a picture and in speech; the book gives the full argument, the references and the exercises. Chapters appear here as they are written.
